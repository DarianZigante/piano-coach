Piano Coach

Choose a piece and play it on your piano. The computer listens through its microphone, follows you along the sheet music, waits when you pause, and shows you which notes to fix.

Before you start

  1. Set the laptop on or beside the piano.
  2. Press Start listening and allow the microphone.
  3. Keep the room fairly quiet while you play.

Good to know: when two notes are struck at the same moment exactly an octave apart, a microphone cannot always tell whether the upper one was played, so the coach assumes it was. Wrong notes are still caught. Works best in Chrome.
